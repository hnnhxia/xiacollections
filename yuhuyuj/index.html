About Xia

A love story between wire & blooms

Hi! I'm Xia — a self-taught wire artist based in beautiful Cebu, Philippines. What started as a quiet hobby became a passion that I now share with the world through Fuzz & Bloom.

Every piece I create is hand-twisted, petal by petal, using premium copper and silver wire. I believe that beauty doesn't have to fade — these flowers will outlast any season.

The Craft

From spool to bloom

Every piece goes through a meditative four-step ritual before it reaches your hands.

01
Design & Sketch
Each flower begins as a pencil sketch inspired by Xia's morning garden walks.
02
Wire Selection
Premium copper, silver or gold-filled wire is chosen for its gauge and character.
03
Hand Sculpting
Petal by petal, each form is twisted and shaped with jeweller's pliers over 2–6 hours.
04
Finish & Pack
A final treatment seals the metal. Pieces are nestled in kraft boxes with a handwritten note.
